Help & Hardware Documentation

Target Client Hardware Specification

AI Vision Navigator is engineered to execute locally on client hardware without requiring external GPUs:

Intel Core i5 CPU 8 GB System RAM Solid State Drive (SSD) Standard RGB Laptop Webcam Integrated Graphics (CPU-Only Pipeline)

Supported Target Classes

Laptop Computer Mobile Phone Table / Desk Pen Pencil Spectacles Water Bottle Medicine Box Notebook / Book Bag / Backpack Instrumentation Box

Voice Commands Cheat Sheet

🗣️ "Where is my mobile?" / "Find my phone."
🗣️ "Where is my laptop?" / "Where is my computer?"
🗣️ "Find my book." / "Where is my notebook?"
🗣️ "Where is my bag?" / "Find my water bottle."
🗣️ "Where are my spectacles?" / "Find the pen."
🗣️ "Start tracking." / failure "Stop tracking."

On-Device Privacy Architecture

Your webcam feed is analyzed in memory on your device using WebSocket frame streams. Video frames are processed in RAM and discarded immediately. No video images or voice recordings are stored or transmitted.

Safety & Limitations Notice

⚠️ Notice: This application provides experimental AI vision spatial guidance. Monocular depth predictions and object distance labels are approximate. Do not use for safety-critical navigation or obstacle avoidance for visually impaired individuals without human supervision.
